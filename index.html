— LEVEL 1 —
⚔️ DEFENSE vs OFFENSE ⚔️

🎮 THE BIG IDEA

Baseball has two sides — just like two teams in a video game! One team is trying to STOP the other team, and the other team is trying to SCORE.

🛡️
DEFENSE
The team in the FIELD.
They throw, catch, and try to get the other team OUT.

🧤 9 players spread across the field
🎯 Goal: Get 3 outs to end the inning
⚡VS⚡
⚔️
OFFENSE
The team that is BATTING.
They hit the ball and run the bases to score!

🏏 One batter at a time
🎯 Goal: Score as many RUNS as possible

🔄 THEY TAKE TURNS!

Each team gets a turn on offense and a turn on defense. When your team is on the field with gloves — that's DEFENSE. When your team is up to bat — that's OFFENSE.


One time through for BOTH teams = 1 INNING

A game has 6 INNINGS in Little League!

💡
EASY WAY TO REMEMBER: Defense = "De-FENCE" = you're guarding the fence (the field). Offense = "Oh! FENCE!" = you're trying to hit it OVER the fence!

🎮
PRO TIP: The INFIELD (P, C, 1B, 2B, 3B, SS) plays close to the bases. The OUTFIELD (LF, CF, RF) plays on the grass far away. Think of it like the infield is your close-range squad and the outfield is your long-range squad!
— LEVEL 3 —
⚔️ OFFENSE & BATTING ⚔️

🏏 WHAT IS OFFENSE?

When your team is on OFFENSE, you are trying to SCORE RUNS! You score a run by hitting the ball and running around ALL the bases — first, second, third, and back to home plate!

🔄 HOW BATTING WORKS

Your team has a BATTING ORDER — a list that says who bats first, second, third, and so on. You MUST bat in this order, every single time. No skipping!


The pitcher throws the ball. The batter tries to hit it. Here's what can happen:

  • STRIKE — You swing and miss, OR the ball goes over the plate and you don't swing. 3 strikes = YOU'RE OUT!
  • BALL — The pitch is outside the strike zone and you don't swing. 4 balls = FREE WALK to first base!
  • FOUL BALL — You hit the ball but it goes outside the lines. Counts as a strike (but can't strike you out on its own unless you bunt).
  • HIT! — You smack the ball into the field! RUN!! 🏃💨

🏃 RUN THE BASES!

After you hit the ball, you run counterclockwise around the bases:

🏃

Touch ALL 4 bases to score a RUN! ✅

💥 TYPES OF HITS

  • SINGLE — You make it to 1st base
  • DOUBLE — You make it to 2nd base
  • TRIPLE — You make it to 3rd base
  • HOME RUN — You hit it over the fence and run ALL the bases! 🎆 Every runner on base scores too!
⭐
REMEMBER: You can also reach base by getting HIT BY A PITCH (ouch!) or if the catcher messes up a pitch (passed ball/wild pitch). The goal is always the same — GET ON BASE and then GET HOME!
— LEVEL 4 —
📜 RULES OF THE GAME 📜

👆 TAP EACH CARD TO FLIP IT AND LEARN THE RULE!

🤯
DROPPED
THIRD STRIKE
[ TAP ME ]
If you swing at strike three and the CATCHER DROPS THE BALL, it's still in play! You can RUN TO FIRST BASE! The catcher has to throw you out at first. So don't just stand there — RUN! 🏃💨
⚡
FORCE
PLAY
[ TAP ME ]
A FORCE happens when a runner HAS to run to the next base because the batter hit the ball. The fielder just has to touch the base with the ball — no tag needed! Example: runner on 1st, ball is hit → runner MUST go to 2nd.
✋
DEAD
BALL
[ TAP ME ]
When the umpire calls "TIME" or the ball goes out of play (like into the dugout), the ball is DEAD. No one can run or be tagged out. Everything pauses until the umpire says "PLAY!" again.
🏷️
TAG
PLAY
[ TAP ME ]
When a runner is NOT forced (doesn't HAVE to run), the fielder must TAG the runner with the ball or glove (with ball in it) to get them out. Just touching the base isn't enough — you gotta tag 'em!
✈️
FLY
BALL OUT
[ TAP ME ]
If a fielder catches the ball in the air BEFORE it hits the ground, the batter is OUT! But if runners are on base, they can "tag up" — wait on their base until the ball is caught, THEN run to the next base.
🧊
PITCHER HAS
THE BALL
[ TAP ME ]
When the pitcher has the ball on the mound, runners should STOP and stay on their base. If you try to run when the pitcher has the ball and is on the rubber, you might get called back or tagged out!
● ● ● ● ● ● ● ●

🎯 FORCE PLAY SCENARIOS

Let's look at different situations so you REALLY understand force plays. Tap each one!

🔵 SCENARIO 1: Runner on 1st, ground ball hit to shortstop

The runner on 1st MUST run to 2nd (because the batter is running to 1st and pushing them forward). The shortstop can throw to 2nd base — the fielder just touches the base. That's a FORCE OUT! If they're quick, they can throw to 1st too for a DOUBLE PLAY (two outs!).

▸ tap to reveal
🔵 SCENARIO 2: Runners on 1st and 2nd, ground ball hit

ALL runners must advance! The runner on 2nd is FORCED to 3rd, the runner on 1st is forced to 2nd, and the batter goes to 1st. Any base can be a force out here — the fielder just needs to touch the base before the runner gets there.

▸ tap to reveal
🔵 SCENARIO 3: Runner on 2nd ONLY, ground ball hit

The runner on 2nd does NOT have to run! No one is behind them pushing them forward. The batter is forced to run to 1st (that's always a force). But the runner on 2nd can choose to stay. If they DO run, they must be TAGGED — not a force!

▸ tap to reveal
🔵 SCENARIO 4: Bases loaded (runners on 1st, 2nd, AND 3rd)

EVERY runner is forced! The fielder can step on ANY base for a force out. This is the easiest force play situation because every base is a force. Home plate too — the catcher can just step on home plate to get the runner from 3rd out!

▸ tap to reveal
🧠
THE GOLDEN RULE OF FORCE PLAYS: A runner is forced ONLY when every base behind them (toward home) is occupied AND the batter is running. Think of it like a traffic jam — you HAVE to move if someone is pushing from behind!
